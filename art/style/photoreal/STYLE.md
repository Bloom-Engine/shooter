# Photoreal concept prompts

One folder per asset: `prompt.txt` (verbatim prompt) and `front-N.png` (generated concept).
Regenerate with `tools/gen/codex_image.sh art/style/photoreal/<name>/front-1.png 1024x1536 art/style/photoreal/<name>/prompt.txt`.

All creature prompts share one species-family paragraph and all gear prompts share one hardware paragraph so the cast matches. The creatures are original designs for this game (the earlier Unvanquished-derived models are being retired).
