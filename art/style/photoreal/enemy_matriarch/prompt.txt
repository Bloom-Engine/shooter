Design: the brood mother of the family, a gaunt giant about 4.5 m tall and unlike the squat heavy ones: a long narrow torso with a deep keeled ribcage, very long lean arms that reach to its knees and end in four long hooked fingers, digitigrade-looking but plantigrade legs with heavy shins, and a long skull drawn back into a tall fan-shaped crest of seven overlapping chitin blades like a crown. Six small amber eyes in two rows of three, a wide lipless mouth of small peg teeth, a pale ribbed throat pouch under the jaw. Across the upper back and the backs of the shoulders sit clusters of large pale translucent brood sacs like leathery eggs half sunk in the hide, some visible from the front above the shoulders. The hide is paler than the others, bone-ochre going to slate at the limbs, stretched thin over the ribs. Old, deliberate, regal.

Shared look of this species family: believable alien wildlife from one ecosystem, grounded in real animal anatomy. Thick leathery hide in desaturated slate grey and bone-ochre with darker mottling along the spine, a damp natural sheen, visible pores, scars, wrinkles at the joints and fine veins; overlapping plates of dark brown-black chitin, scuffed and chipped at the edges, growing out of the skin on the head, shoulders, forearms and shins; small deep-set amber eyes; blunt keratin claws, yellowed at the tips. No clothing, no armour, no technology, no weapons, no glowing magic, no horns like a demon, no wings, no tail.
