Bloom Shooter is a third-person arena shooter. The swarm comes in waves, and you are the only thing standing in its way.

Fight through five handcrafted arenas, ten waves each, from a plaza in the late-afternoon sun to a misty river ravine, a checkpoint at dusk, an overcast quarry and, at night, Brood Hollow, where the Matriarch waits for you.

SEVEN KINDS OF ALIEN, ONE MATRIARCH
Skitters swarm, Stalkers close in fast, Ravagers rush you, Bruisers hit hard and Colossi take a beating, while Spitters and Bombards strike from range. Each one sees, hears and hunts you on its own terms. Elites are bigger and tougher, and the Matriarch carries her weak point on her back.

SIX WEAPONS, EVERY MOVE YOU NEED
Rifle, blaster, chaingun, cannon, scattergun and lance. Aim down sights, sprint, crouch, vault over cover and roll out of danger. Swap the camera to your other shoulder, throw grenades, and knock enemies back with a rifle-butt strike.

MORE THAN SURVIVAL
Some waves ask you to hold a position or burn out alien nests. Earn credits and stars, then upgrade your weapons between runs.

BUILT TO LOOK AND SOUND REAL
Photoreal creatures and gear, dynamic lighting, a different time of day in every arena, 3D positional sound, and a soundtrack that rises with the fight.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Touch controls made for phones and tablets
• Easy, Normal and Hard
• Subtitles and colour palettes for red-green and blue-yellow colour vision deficiency, plus high contrast
• In-game text in English, German, French and Spanish
